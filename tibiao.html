Tibiao, Antique

Overview

Tibiao is a municipality in Antique known for its eco-tourism and outdoor adventure experiences. It has gained popularity for offering unique activities such as kawa hot baths, river tubing, and trekking.

Surrounded by lush forests, rivers, and mountains, Tibiao provides visitors with a refreshing escape into nature. It is an ideal destination for those seeking both relaxation and thrilling outdoor activities.

Its combination of natural beauty and cultural experiences makes Tibiao one of the most exciting destinations in Antique.

Natural Features

Tibiao is rich in natural attractions, including rivers, waterfalls, and forested mountains. Its rivers are known for their clean and flowing waters, making them ideal for water-based adventures.

The surrounding landscapes offer scenic views and a peaceful environment, perfect for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

The area promotes eco-tourism, encouraging visitors to appreciate and preserve its natural environment while enjoying outdoor activities.

Things to Do

  • Kawa Bath – Experience a relaxing traditional hot bath.
  • River Tubing – Ride along the river using rubber tubes.
  • Trekking – Explore forest trails and mountain paths.
  • Waterfall Visits – Discover scenic waterfalls.
  • Camping – Enjoy outdoor stays in nature.
  • Photography – Capture rivers, forests, and landscapes.

Travel Information

Tibiao is accessible by land from San Jose de Buenavista, Antique’s capital, with a travel time of approximately 1–2 hours.

The best time to visit is during the dry season, from November to May, when outdoor activities are safer and more enjoyable.

Visitors are advised to bring extra clothes, wear comfortable attire, and follow safety guidelines when participating in adventure activities.
