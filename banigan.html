Banigan Festival

Overview

Banigan Festival celebrates Antique’s rich tradition of weaving banig (handwoven mats), showcasing the creativity and craftsmanship of local artisans.

The festival is held in the municipality of Anini-y and highlights the importance of weaving in the local economy and culture.

It promotes heritage preservation through artistic expression and community participation.

Festival Features

The festival features colorful costumes inspired by woven patterns and materials.

Street dancing performances highlight weaving techniques and cultural stories.

Exhibits and workshops demonstrate traditional craftsmanship.
