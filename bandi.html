Bandi

Overview

Bandi is a well-known delicacy in Antique, made from roasted peanuts mixed with caramelized sugar. It is shaped into bars or blocks and is popular as a snack or souvenir.

The process of making Bandi involves carefully roasting peanuts and combining them with melted sugar to achieve its signature crunch and sweetness.

It is widely sold in local markets and is often enjoyed by both locals and visitors.

Taste & Texture

Bandi is known for its crunchy texture and rich peanut flavor combined with sweetness from caramelized sugar.

Its simple ingredients make it a classic treat that highlights traditional snack-making techniques.

It is often packaged and sold as a pasalubong for visitors.
