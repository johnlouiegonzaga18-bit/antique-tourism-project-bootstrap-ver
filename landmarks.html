Landmarks of Antique

Discover historic and cultural sites across the province

Historic & Cultural Heritage

Antique is home to several historical and religious landmarks that reflect the province’s rich cultural heritage and long-standing traditions.

These landmarks showcase Spanish-era architecture, local craftsmanship, and the strong influence of faith in the daily lives of the people.

Visitors can explore these sites to gain a deeper understanding of Antique’s history and identity.
