Festivals of Antique

Experience the vibrant culture and traditions of the province

Discover Antique Festivals

Antique is home to colorful festivals that showcase the province’s rich history, traditions, and strong community spirit. These celebrations highlight local culture through music, dance, costumes, and religious devotion.

Each festival reflects a unique story of the people of Antique, combining historical significance with lively performances and cultural expressions.

Visitors can immerse themselves in these events and experience the warmth and hospitality of the locals.

Featured Festivals

Binirayan Festival

The oldest festival in Antique celebrating the arrival of Malay settlers.

Banigan Festival

A celebration of Antique’s traditional weaving industry.

Pahinis Festival

A unique festival featuring traditional body cleansing rituals.
