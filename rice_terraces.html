Rice Terraces of Antique

Overview

The rice terraces of Antique are a testament to the ingenuity and hard work of local farmers. Carved into the mountains, these terraces provide both livelihood and breathtaking scenery.

They reflect the province’s agricultural heritage and sustainable farming practices passed down through generations.

Visitors can enjoy scenic views while learning about traditional farming culture.

Natural Features

The terraces are built along mountain slopes, forming layered landscapes that follow the natural contour of the land.

During planting season, the fields turn vibrant green, while harvest season gives a golden appearance.

These landscapes are both functional and visually stunning.

Highlights

Scenic Views

Beautiful mountain landscapes.

Photography Spot

Quiet and relaxing place.

Things to Do

  • Sightseeing – Enjoy the terrace views.
  • Photography – Capture landscapes.
  • Hiking – Explore nearby trails.
  • Learn Farming – Observe local practices.

Travel Information

The rice terraces are located in upland areas of Antique and are accessible via local transport.

Best visited during planting or harvest seasons for the best views.

Wear comfortable footwear when exploring the area.
