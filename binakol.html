Binakol

Overview

Binakol is a traditional Filipino chicken soup that originates from the Visayas region, including Antique. It is known for its unique preparation using coconut water instead of regular broth, giving it a distinct and refreshing flavor.

The dish is often cooked with native chicken, ginger, lemongrass, and sometimes green papaya or chayote. It is typically prepared inside bamboo, which enhances its aroma and taste.

Binakol is commonly served during family gatherings and special occasions, making it both a comforting and culturally significant dish.

Flavor & Preparation

Binakol is known for its light yet flavorful broth, infused with the natural sweetness of coconut water and the aroma of herbs.

Cooking it in bamboo adds a subtle earthy flavor, making it distinct from other chicken soups.

The combination of ingredients results in a nourishing and refreshing dish.
