Hamtic Historical Area

Overview

Hamtic is one of the oldest municipalities in Antique and is considered the province’s historical capital. It played an important role during the Spanish colonial period and remains a significant cultural center today.

The area is known for its historical landmarks, churches, and community traditions that reflect centuries of development and heritage.

Visitors can explore a blend of history, culture, and modern local life in this important municipality.

Historical Importance

Hamtic was once the center of governance during colonial times, making it a key location in Antique’s history.

It is home to historical churches and structures that showcase Spanish influence and local craftsmanship.

Today, it continues to preserve its heritage while serving as a thriving municipality.
