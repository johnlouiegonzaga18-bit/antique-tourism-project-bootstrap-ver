St. John Nepomucene Parish Church (Anini-y)

Overview

The St. John Nepomucene Parish Church in Anini-y is one of the oldest and most historically significant religious landmarks in Antique. Established during the Spanish colonial period, the church reflects centuries of faith, resilience, and cultural heritage of the local community.

Known for its massive stone structure and simple yet elegant design, the church stands as a symbol of enduring devotion. Its thick coral stone walls and traditional architecture showcase the craftsmanship of early builders and the influence of Spanish-era construction.

Today, it continues to serve as a place of worship and a cultural landmark, attracting both pilgrims and tourists interested in history and architecture.

Architectural Features

The church features a classic baroque-inspired façade with a triangular pediment and a sturdy bell tower. Its structure is built using coral stones and lime mortar, giving it both durability and historical authenticity.

Inside, visitors can find religious icons, wooden altars, and a peaceful atmosphere that reflects centuries of continuous use as a sacred space.

The surrounding area includes an open courtyard and views of nearby coastal landscapes, adding to its serene and reflective environment.
