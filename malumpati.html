Malumpati Cold Spring

Overview

Malumpati Cold Spring is one of Antique’s most popular natural attractions, located in the municipality of Pandan. It is known for its crystal-clear and refreshing waters that flow directly from the mountains.

The spring offers a relaxing environment surrounded by lush greenery, making it a favorite destination for families and tourists seeking a cool escape from the heat.

Its natural beauty and clean waters make it an ideal place for swimming, relaxation, and nature appreciation.

Natural Features

Malumpati is famous for its cool, crystal-clear spring water flowing through a wide river basin. The surrounding area is covered with trees and natural rock formations.

The river’s depth varies, making it suitable for both shallow wading and deeper swimming areas.

Its clean environment reflects the community’s efforts to preserve its natural condition.

Things to Do

  • Swimming – Enjoy the cold spring waters.
  • Relaxation – Rest under shaded areas.
  • Picnic – Spend time with family and friends.
  • Photography – Capture nature scenes.
  • River Exploration – Walk along the riverbanks.

Travel Information

Malumpati is accessible via land travel from Pandan, Antique. Local transportation and tricycles are available to reach the site.

The best time to visit is during dry months when water conditions are calm.

Visitors are encouraged to follow cleanliness rules to preserve the area.
